# Magic Gift Tower

### Rulebook v1.2

*A fast-paced family board game of public auctions, magical gifts, and pattern building.*

---

# 1. Story

Once every century, the legendary **Magic Gift Tower** awakens.

Deep within the tower lie magical gifts waiting to be revealed. Wizards from across the kingdom gather to make offerings of Gold, hoping the Tower will grant the gifts they desire.

The cleverest wizard will create the most powerful magical formation—or even achieve the legendary perfect collection.

---

# 2. Objective

Collect Magic Gift Dice through public auctions and arrange them on your personal **3×3 board** to create magical formations.

At the end of the game, score Victory Points from your completed formations and convert your remaining Gold into additional Victory Points.

Alternatively, complete the **Legendary Formation** to instantly win the game.

---

# 3. Players

* **2–5 Players**
* **Age:** 8+
* **Play Time:** 20–30 Minutes
* **Complexity:** ★★☆☆☆–★★★☆☆ (Weight ~2.0)

---

# 4. Components

* 1 Magic Gift Tower
* **9 Magic Gift Dice per player**
* Gold Tokens
* 1 Player Board (3×3 Grid) per player
* Score Pad

---

# 5. Magic Gift Dice

Each die has six magical gift icons instead of numbers.

Example icons:

* 🔥 Fire
* 💎 Crystal
* 🌙 Moon
* 🌿 Nature
* 👑 Crown
* ⭐ Star

All dice share the same six icons.

---

# 6. Setup

1. Prepare **9 dice per player**.

| Players | Dice |
| ------: | ---: |
|       2 |   18 |
|       3 |   27 |
|       4 |   36 |
|       5 |   45 |

2. Shuffle all dice into a common supply.

3. Each player receives:

* 30 Gold
* 1 random die
* 1 Player Board

4. Each player chooses any face of their starting die and places it face up into **any space** of their board.

The starting die is public information.

Once placed, a die may never be moved or rotated.

5. Return all remaining dice to the common supply.

6. Randomly choose the first player.

---

# 7. Game End

The game ends immediately if either condition is met:

### Legendary Formation

If a player fills all nine spaces with the **same icon**, they immediately win the game.

Final scoring is skipped.

### Normal End

When a player places their **ninth die**, finish the current round so every player may place any dice they won during that round.

Then proceed to Final Scoring.

Unused dice remaining in the supply are discarded.

---

# 8. Round Structure

Each round consists of four phases.

---

## Phase 1 – Public Offering

Before any dice are rolled, every player publicly places Gold onto any of the six Gift Icons.

The six icon auctions are:

* 🔥 Fire
* 💎 Crystal
* 🌙 Moon
* 🌿 Nature
* 👑 Crown
* ⭐ Star

Players may:

* Bid on one icon.
* Bid on multiple icons.
* Skip any icon.
* Keep unspent Gold.

### Bid Cap Rule

**The sum of all of a player's bids across all six icons this round may never exceed that player's current Gold on hand.**

A player only pays for icons they actually win (see Phase 3), but they must have enough Gold to cover every bid *simultaneously* at the moment bids are placed — a player cannot commit more Gold across their bids than they physically have, even if they expect to lose most of those auctions.

### Unique Bid Rule

For each icon, every bid must be unique.

Example (Valid)

| Player | Fire Bid |
| ------ | -------: |
| Alice  |        7 |
| Ben    |        5 |
| Chris  |        2 |

Example (Invalid)

| Player | Fire Bid |
| ------ | -------: |
| Alice  |        6 |
| Ben    |        6 |

### Tie Resolution Order

If two or more players submit matching bids on the same icon, resolve in **seating order starting from the current first player**:

1. The player closest to (and including) the first player, in turn order, keeps their bid unchanged.
2. Each subsequent tied player, in turn order, must immediately either raise their bid to a new unique value or withdraw their bid on that icon entirely.
3. Once a player withdraws or raises to a unique value, move to the next tied player, if any, and repeat.
4. This resolves in a single pass — no back-and-forth counter-raising. A player who withdraws may not re-bid on that icon this round.

All bid adjustments still count against the Bid Cap Rule above.

Gold remains committed until the round is resolved.

## Phase 2 – Roll the Magic Gift Tower

Roll **one die per player** through the Magic Gift Tower.

Example (4 Players)

Results:

* 🔥
* 🔥
* 👑
* 🌙

This round produces:

* Fire ×2
* Crown ×1
* Moon ×1

---

## Phase 3 – Resolve the Auctions

Resolve each icon separately.

For every die showing an icon, award one die to the highest remaining bidder for that icon.

Example

Fire ×2

| Player | Bid |
| ------ | --: |
| Alice  |   8 |
| Ben    |   6 |
| Chris  |   3 |

Result

* Alice receives one Fire die.
* Ben receives one Fire die.
* Chris receives nothing.

Only players who successfully receive a die pay their bid.

Players who receive nothing pay nothing.

### Unclaimed Dice

**If a rolled die's icon received no bids at all this round, that die is returned to the common supply and remains available for future rounds.** It is not discarded.

---

## Phase 4 – Place Dice

Players who received dice place them onto any empty spaces of their boards.

The visible face must match the icon they received.

Once placed:

* Dice may never move.
* Dice may never rotate.

If a player completes the Legendary Formation, they immediately win the game.

Otherwise, begin the next round.

---

# 9. Final Scoring

If no player achieved the Legendary Formation, calculate scores.

## General Rules

* **Horizontal and vertical** lines are considered for all formations. **Diagonal lines count only for the Three Identical Icons formation** — no other formation may use a diagonal.

### The One Rule (Locking)

> **Each die may be claimed by at most one Line formation *and* at most one Square formation. It may never be claimed twice by the same formation shape.**

That's the entire locking system. Two shape categories, and each die gets one "use" per category:

* **Line formations** — Three Identical, Five of Six Different, Six Identical, and Crossing — all run along rows, columns, or (for Three Identical only) diagonals. A die used by one Line formation cannot also be used by a *different* Line formation.
* **Square formations** — Four Identical and Four Different — both run along 2×2 blocks. A die used by one Square formation cannot also be used by a *different* Square formation.
* Because Line and Square are different categories, **a die can be used by one of each at the same time** — e.g. a die that's part of a scored row can also be part of a scored 2×2 square.
* **Within Line formations**, if the same set of dice could qualify for more than one tier (for example, six matching dice could be read as either "Six Identical" or as two separate "Three Identical"s), always score the single highest-value tier — never both.
* **Within Square formations**, a single 2×2 block scores as either Four Identical or Four Different, never both, and can't be claimed twice by two overlapping readings of the same four spaces.

## Formation Reference Table

Check the board against every formation below, applying The One Rule as you go.

| # | Formation | Shape | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legendary Formation (all 9 spaces, same icon) | — | Instant win, skip scoring |
| 2 | Crossing, Two Identical Icons | Line | 15 VP + 12 Gold |
| 3 | Crossing, Two Different Icons | Line | 12 VP + 12 Gold |
| 4 | Six Identical Icons (two rows or two columns) | Line | 9 VP + 9 Gold |
| 5 | Five of Six Different Icons (two rows or two columns; at most one icon repeats) | Line | 9 VP + 9 Gold |
| 6 | Three Identical Icons (row, column, **or diagonal**) | Line | 3 VP + 3 Gold |
| 7 | Four Identical Icons (2×2 block) | Square | 5 VP + 5 Gold |
| 8 | Four Different Icons (2×2 block) | Square | 3 VP + 3 Gold |

A board can score several formations in the same pass — e.g. a Crossing Formation (Line) plus a 2×2 Square that happens to share a die with it, since Line and Square are separate categories under The One Rule.

## Crossing Formation

A Crossing Formation consists of:

* One complete horizontal line.
* One complete vertical line.
* The two lines intersect at the center space.

### Two Different Icons

Example

```text
🔥 🔥 🔥
🌙 🔥 🌙
🌙 🔥 🌙
```

Scores: **12 VP + 12 Gold**

The row and column do not additionally score as Three Identical Icons — under The One Rule, these dice already used their Line-formation slot on the Crossing.

### Two Identical Icons

Example

```text
🔥 🔥 🔥
🔥 🔥 🔥
🔥 🔥 🔥
```

Scores: **15 VP + 12 Gold**

Same rule applies. A 2×2 Square formation may still score separately even if it shares dice with the crossing row or column, since Square is a different category under The One Rule.

## Gold Conversion

After collecting all Gold rewards, convert your remaining Gold into Victory Points.

**Every 2 Gold = 1 Victory Point** (round down).

Examples:

| Gold | VP |
| ---: | -: |
|    5 |  2 |
|    8 |  4 |
|   11 |  5 |

## Worked Examples

### Example A — Six Identical Icons (no double-scoring)

```text
🔥 🔥 🔥
🔥 🔥 🔥
🌙 🌿 👑
```

* Rows 1 and 2 are both all-Fire → qualifies as **Six Identical Icons** (9 VP + 9 Gold), the single highest-value Line tier these six dice can reach.
* Those same six dice do **not** also score as two separate Three Identical Icons formations — under The One Rule, each die already got its one Line-formation use from Six Identical.
* Row 3 has three different icons — no formation there.
* **Total: 9 VP + 9 Gold.**

### Example B — Five of Six Different + Four Identical Square (Line + Square stacking)

```text
💎 🌙 ⭐
🔥 👑 👑
🌿 👑 👑
```

* **Rows 1–2** (💎 🌙 ⭐ / 🔥 👑 👑): six cells contain 💎, 🌙, ⭐, 🔥, 👑, 👑 — five distinct icons with 👑 repeating once → qualifies as **Five of Six Different Icons** (9 VP + 9 Gold). All six of these dice have now used their one Line-formation slot.
* **Rows 2–3, columns 2–3** (👑👑 / 👑👑): all four cells are 👑 → qualifies as a **Four Identical Icons** square (5 VP + 5 Gold). Two of these dice (from row 2) already scored a Line formation above — but Square is a separate category under The One Rule, so their Square-slot is still available.
* **Total: 9 + 5 = 14 VP, 9 + 5 = 14 Gold.**

### Example C — Diagonal Three Identical + Four Different Square

```text
🔥 💎 🌿
🌙 🔥 ⭐
👑 🌿 🔥
```

* The **main diagonal** (top-left to bottom-right: 🔥/🔥/🔥) is all Fire → qualifies as **Three Identical Icons** (3 VP + 3 Gold) — legal because diagonals count for this formation. These three dice have now used their Line-formation slot.
* **Rows 1–2, columns 2–3** (💎🌿 / 🔥⭐): four different icons (💎, 🌿, 🔥, ⭐) → qualifies as a **Four Different Icons** square (3 VP + 3 Gold). It shares one die (🔥) with the diagonal above, but Square is a separate category under The One Rule, so that die's Square-slot is still free.
* **Total: 3 + 3 = 6 VP, 6 Gold.**

---

# 10. Winner

The player with the highest total Victory Points wins.

If there is a tie:

1. The tied player with the most remaining Gold wins.
2. If still tied, all tied players share the victory.

## Worked Example — Full Final Scoring (3 Players)

The game reaches Normal End. No one completed the Legendary Formation. Here are the three final boards, along with each player's remaining Gold after all rounds of bidding.

### Alice's Board — 11 Gold remaining

```text
🔥 🔥 🔥
🔥 🔥 🔥
🌙 🌿 👑
```

* Rows 1–2 are both all-Fire → **Six Identical Icons**: 9 VP + 9 Gold.
* Row 3 has no formation.
* **Formation total: 9 VP + 9 Gold.**
* Gold after formations: 11 (remaining) + 9 (formation reward) = **20 Gold.**
* Gold Conversion: 20 ÷ 2 = **10 VP** (round down).
* **Alice's total score: 9 + 10 = 19 VP.**

### Ben's Board — 6 Gold remaining

```text
💎 🌙 ⭐
🔥 👑 👑
🌿 👑 👑
```

* Rows 1–2 → **Five of Six Different Icons** (💎, 🌙, ⭐, 🔥, 👑 — five distinct, 👑 repeats once): 9 VP + 9 Gold.
* Rows 2–3, columns 2–3, all 👑 → **Four Identical Icons** square: 5 VP + 5 Gold. (Legal alongside the Line formation above under The One Rule — Line and Square are separate categories.)
* **Formation total: 9 + 5 = 14 VP, 9 + 5 = 14 Gold.**
* Gold after formations: 6 (remaining) + 14 (formation reward) = **20 Gold.**
* Gold Conversion: 20 ÷ 2 = **10 VP.**
* **Ben's total score: 14 + 10 = 24 VP.**

### Chris's Board — 17 Gold remaining

```text
🔥 💎 🌿
🌙 🔥 ⭐
👑 🌿 🔥
```

* Main diagonal (🔥/🔥/🔥) → **Three Identical Icons**: 3 VP + 3 Gold.
* Rows 1–2, columns 2–3 (💎🌿 / 🔥⭐, all different) → **Four Different Icons** square: 3 VP + 3 Gold.
* **Formation total: 3 + 3 = 6 VP, 6 Gold.**
* Gold after formations: 17 (remaining) + 6 (formation reward) = **23 Gold.**
* Gold Conversion: 23 ÷ 2 = 11.5 → round down → **11 VP.**
* **Chris's total score: 6 + 11 = 17 VP.**

### Final Standings

| Player | Formation VP | Formation Gold | Gold Remaining Before | Total Gold | Gold → VP | **Total Score** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Ben   |  14 |  14 |  6 | 20 | 10 | **24 VP** |
| Alice |   9 |   9 | 11 | 20 | 10 | **19 VP** |
| Chris |   6 |   6 | 17 | 23 | 11 | **17 VP** |

**Ben wins with 24 VP.**

Note how the Gold Conversion step matters just as much as the formations themselves: Alice's single high-value Line formation and Ben's stacked Line+Square combo both converted to the same 20 Gold → 10 VP, but Ben's formations were worth more VP outright, which is what separated the two. Chris, despite the smallest formation haul, banked the most leftover Gold from cautious bidding — enough to nearly catch up, but not quite.

# Design Philosophy

Every round of **Magic Gift Tower** revolves around three simple decisions:

* **Predict** which magical gifts the Tower will reveal.
* **Invest** enough Gold to outbid your rivals without overspending.
* **Arrange** your gifts to create powerful formations on your 3×3 board.

The rules are intentionally easy to teach, while the public auctions, unpredictable dice tower, and satisfying pattern-building create exciting moments for both families and casual gamers.
